Alien Invaders

Designing three levels that feel interconnected and reward player exploration.

Back to Projects

Project Overview

Project: Alien Invaders, level design

My Roles: Level Design, Player Progression, Play Testing

Tools: Platformer Engine by Cowsins

Timeline: 4 weeks

For this project, I designed a three-section, 2D platforming experience inspired by Hollow Knight and Celeste. My goal was to create an interconnected world that encouraged exploration, rewarded curiosity, and gradually challenged players as they learned new mechanics. The experience follows a player trapped in a crater created by an alien invasion. To escape, the player must descend into an alien facility, obtain a grappling hook, and use their new abilities to navigate previously inaccessible areas and reach the surface. Rather than treating each section as a separate level, I designed the environment around a continuous progression loop. Players descend into the facility, gain a new ability, and retrace their steps to discover new paths before facing a final platforming challenge.

Design Process

Design Goals

Interconnected Level Design

I wanted players to feel that they were exploring one cohesive world rather than a series of disconnected stages, as well as encourage exploration.

Progressive Difficulty

Introduce mechanics gradually before combining them into more difficult sequences.

Player Choice

Incorporate multiple routes, optional rewards, and weapon choices to give players some control over how they explored and approached challenges.

>

Initial Design

Section 1 - The Underground

The opening section introduces the player to the environment and basic platforming mechanics. I designed branching cave pathways, optional exploration routes, and areas that players could see but could not yet access.

Section 2 - The Alien Facility

The second section shifts to a more artificial environment, using angular layouts, winding routes, enemies, and moving platforms to create a distinct identity. This is where players obtain the grappling hook, unlocking a new way to navigate the world.

Section 3 - The Surface

The final section focuses on mastering the grappling hook. I combined grappling challenges with bounce pads to create a more demanding platforming sequence that tests the skills players developed earlier.

Ideal Path

Playtest 1

What players liked

Players liked the idea of getting the grappler in level 2 and re-expliring level 1 with the new tool. They also liked the distinct theme of each level to distinguish them from eachother.

What could improve

Players felt that it was too open and easy to get lost, so they didn't venture out and explore much. While they liked the idea of different themese for different sections, they felt it could be displayed more aesthically.

Design response
  • Added more platforming to the center of section 1 and reworked the moving platforms in section 2 to bring the player to different spaces and fix the feeling of emptiness.
  • Created rule tiles and made each section fit their theme visually.
  • Added more pickups on alternate paths to encourage more payer exploration.
  • Extnded section 3 to include more of a challenge and make reahcing the end feel more rewarding.

Beta

Playtest 2

What players liked

The additional rooms at the end were challenging, but it made completing the level feel rewarding. There was nowhere they could softlock themselves and the level felt fluid to progress through.

What could improve

The additional rooms at the end were very challenging to the point of being frustrating for some less experienced players. Players who didn't know a lot about the engine had a hard time figuring out how to solve the final puzzle and felt overwhelmed in the last combat room.

Design response plan
  • Add more rooms introducing players to the physics engine/multiple pucks in Sokoban puzzles so they feel more equipped to deal with the final puzzle.
  • Space out the enemies more in the final combat room and give the player more space to move so they feel less overwhelmed.
