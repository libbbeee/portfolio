Project Overview

Project: Monarch, a 2D metroidvania

My Roles: Game Designer, Systems Designer, Writer, Artist

Timeline: 3 weeks

Monarch is a 2D metroidvania, where the player represents the Young Girl living in a world much bigger than herself. The player's goal is to explore the world through a combination of platforming and combat, gaining more control over their abilities and surroundings as they play. This game blends the popular ability based progression gameplay seen in classic metroidvanias like Hollow Knight, with a deeper focus on storytelling like Journey and Gris.

My Contributions

Game Design

The game design document I wrote for Monarch details a complete concept for the 2D Metroidvania game. It includes the design pillars, core gameplay, character design and more. The document establishes a clear direction for the game before the start of development.

Game Writing

A large part of the game design document is dedicated to establishing the world of Monarch. It details the tone, aesthetics, and story that the player will be immersed in when playing the game.
