Project Overview

Project: A Ripple of Change, a web-clicker game

My Roles: Game Designer, Systems Designer, Game Developer

Timeline: 3 weeks

Ripple of Change is a simple, engaging mobile game inspired by charity:water's mission of bringing clean water to communities around the world. Players begin with a polluted lake filled with trash and murky water. By tapping the charity:water jerry can, they generate "Vitality Points" that can be used to clean the environment, restore wildlife, and build community resources such as gardens and wells. The core appeal of the game is watching the environment transform over time. As players invest their points, the water becomes clearer, trash disappears, fish return, and the surrounding community thrives. This visual progression creates a rewarding gameplay loop while reinforcing the message that small actions can create meaningful change.
